# Chapter Real Estate — Website Changes

A plain-language summary of what changed and what still needs input from the team.

---

## New pages

- **Buyer's Guide** — a dedicated page walking buyers through the whole journey in **10 clear steps**
  (research → budget → mortgage → agent → viewings → offer → inspection → financing → appraisal →
  closing), each with its own section and image. Rebranded for Winnipeg/Manitoba. Linked from the
  Brokerage page and footer.
- **Sellers page** — how Chapter sells your home: protecting your sale, around-the-clock support,
  connecting with an agent, negotiation, plus what's included for listing support and marketing.
  Rebranded for Manitoba, using the correct local regulators. Linked from the Brokerage page and footer.

---

## Page-by-page updates

**Header**
- Social media icons moved to a slim bar **above** the main menu so the menu row isn't crowded.
- Fixed spacing so the menu links and Contact / Book buttons sit together neatly.

**Home & Brokerage**
- Removed the "Market Insights" section.
- **Buy** and **Sell** are now short summaries that link to the full Buyer's Guide and Sellers pages.
- **Featured Listings** shows all our office listings with filters. (Official MLS/IDX feed is a future step.)
- **Our Agents** now has a **search box and specialty filter**. (Showing placeholder agents for now.)

**Property Management**
- New headline **"The Chapter Advantage for Landlords"** with three strengths (Expertise & Experience,
  Maximize Rental Income, Personalized Service).
- **"Chapter's Process"** — a 10-step overview written to emphasise it's **simple and hands-off**,
  with a subtle animated background accent.
- **Available Rentals** — residential and commercial units combined into one filterable, listings-style
  section on a lightly shaded background.
- Kept the landlord enquiry form and the Owner/Tenant portals; **removed** the old "Services" and
  "Pricing" sections.

**Investments**
- Corrected to reflect that **Chapter is part of the Barso Group** (with Barso Homes, Barso Concrete,
  BGC Developments, Chapter Property Management), the real mission, and signature developments
  (128 River Avenue, 417 Beaverbrook Street). Investor Relations left unchanged pending direction.

**About**
- New hero background image.
- Replaced the old staff grid with a focused **Leadership** section: **Armin Barsomian — Founder &
  Managing Partner; Dylan — Founder & Partner; Avanish — Broker & Partner.**
- Bigger, bolder **"Our Values"** section (six values, larger icons and type).
